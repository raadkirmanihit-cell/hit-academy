06Final check before I walk in

My ideal, walk-away point and trade-offs are written down.
My BATNA is real and tested, and I know how I'll signal it.
I know their priorities, constraints, style and likely BATNA.
I have positive, negative and normative leverage ready.
I have planned for obstacles and know where I can be flexible.
I have ideas for creating mutual value beyond price.
I have planned the order of topics and how I'll open.
Logistics are set, and I will keep detailed notes.
