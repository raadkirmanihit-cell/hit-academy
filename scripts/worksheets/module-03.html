Warning signs of a counterfeit yes: said quickly or too smoothly · enthusiasm that doesn't match body language · no follow-up questions · a history of agreeing but not delivering · vague answers when asked for details.

02Commitment questions

Script the three steps you will use to turn a yes into a commitment.

1 · Follow-up question ("What specific actions are we agreeing to take moving forward?")

2 · Probe for hesitation ("You seem unsure. Can we address any concerns?")

3 · Small, immediate action that tests the agreement (meeting, PO, letter of intent, draft)
