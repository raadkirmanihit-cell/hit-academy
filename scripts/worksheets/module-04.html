03People and perception prep

Separate the people from the problem before you walk in.

StepMy notes
1 · Consider perception
How do they see this? What am I assuming about their intentions out of fear? Am I blaming them for my problem?
2 · Acknowledge emotion
What are they likely to feel? What do I feel? How will I let them let off steam, without reacting?
3 · Soft communication
Two "I" statements I will use instead of "you" statements. What is the purpose of what I say?

04Options brainstorm: invent, then judge

Invent first: write at least eight options without judging any of them. Then judge: tick the ones that meet my interests, theirs, or both. Options that tick both are your mutual gains.
