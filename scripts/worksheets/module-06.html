07Integrity check

Every loss, deadline and scarcity I mention is real.
My anchor is backed by data I can explain.
Each option I offer is genuinely good for them too.
My questions are sincere, and my gestures expect nothing in return.
I would be comfortable if they knew exactly how I prepared.
