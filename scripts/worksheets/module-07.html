Objection Planner

03Warning-sign checklist

Keep this page next to you. Tick each sign you notice during the negotiation.

Pressure
Ultimatum or threat: "final offer", "we'll cancel"
False deadline: no reason, or it shifts
Outburst: yelling, "This is outrageous!"
Silence: a long pause, a blank stare
Flinch: a gasp, "That's way too high!"
Numbers and trades
Highball / lowball: far outside the norms
Bracketing: new ranges, midpoint logic
Bogey: a minor issue overemphasised
Nibbling: "Just one more thing…"
Cherry picking: the discount without the commitment
Distraction and confusion
Red herring: topic changes when progress is made
Strawman: "So you're saying…" plus exaggeration
Playing dumb: the same basic questions again
Overwhelm: giant packets, jargon
Delay: "still waiting on sign-off"
Authority, teams and closing
Good cop / bad cop: one harsh, one warm
Limited authority: "I need to check with my manager."
Higher authority: "Legal won't approve that."
Divide and conquer: "You seem more reasonable…"
Last-minute change: "Before we sign, just add…"
When you spot one: stay calm · name it or ask about it · return to the facts, the real decision-makers and the timeline.

07Integrity check for my own tactics

Every deadline and consequence I mention is real, and I'm prepared to follow through.
Any issue I emphasise is something I genuinely value. I haven't invented a dealbreaker.
My ambitious numbers are justifiable, and I can explain them.
Any emotion I show is genuine. I'm not exploiting someone vulnerable.
I would be comfortable if they knew exactly how I prepared.
